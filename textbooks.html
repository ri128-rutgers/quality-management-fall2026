Textbooks and library access

What to buy, what to borrow, and how to get it through Rutgers University Libraries

Required

Farr, J. V., & Faber, I. J. (2023). Engineering Economics of Life Cycle Cost Analysis (2nd ed.). CRC Press. ISBN 9781032184876 (paperback); e-book ISBN 9781003254782, DOI 10.1201/9781003254782. Publisher page. Cited as FF.

Chapters 1–14 are assigned across the term. Buy the paperback or e-book, or check e-book access through the library (below).

Backup text (recommended)

Sullivan, W. G., Wicks, E. M., & Wilck, J. H. (2025). Engineering Economy (18th ed.). Pearson. ISBN 9780138075965. Publisher page. Cited as SWW.

Used for foundational chapters (4–10, 12, 14) and practice problems. Any edition from the 16th (2014) onward is usable; chapter numbering is stable across those editions. A Rutgers note: co-author Elin Wicks earned her BS and MS in Industrial Engineering at Rutgers.

Supplemental

  • Baley, I., & Veldkamp, L. (2025). The Data Economy: Tools and Applications. Princeton University Press. ISBN 9780691256726 — data-as-capital models (Weeks 10 and 12).
  • James, G., Witten, D., Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R., & Taylor, J. (2023). An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in Python. Springer. Free at statlearning.com.
  • Agrawal, A., Gans, J., & Goldfarb, A. (2022). Power and Prediction. Harvard Business Review Press.

Getting the books through Rutgers University Libraries

All three commercial titles are on the course reading list in Canvas (the Reading List tool in the left-hand course menu, provided by the Libraries through Leganto). Open a title there to see the library's current access: e-book link, print copy and call number, or a reserve copy. Sign in with your NetID.

Direct checks:

  1. QuickSearch. Go to libraries.rutgers.edu, sign in, and search by ISBN: 9781032184876 (Farr & Faber), 9780138075965 (Sullivan, Wicks & Wilck), 9780691256726 (Baley & Veldkamp). An ISBN search returns the exact edition, including any licensed e-book.
  2. E-book platforms. Farr & Faber is published by CRC Press and appears on Taylor & Francis eBooks when licensed; Baley & Veldkamp is Princeton University Press and appears on De Gruyter or ProQuest Ebook Central when licensed. Pearson textbooks such as Sullivan, Wicks & Wilck are generally not licensed to libraries as e-books, so expect a print or reserve copy.
  3. Course reserves. If a title shows no e-book, a reserve copy is placed at the campus library named in the Reading List record for short-term loan; request delivery to another campus library from the item's QuickSearch record.
  4. Interlibrary loan. Anything not held at Rutgers can be requested through ILL from your QuickSearch account; allow one to two weeks.
  5. Chapter scans. The Libraries will scan one chapter per title for the reading list under their copyright guidance. Ask the instructor before the week you need it.

If a link in the reading list fails or a title shows no access, message the instructor in Canvas and email the Libraries reserves team; the reading list is checked before each week opens.

Free materials used in the course

All government guidance (GAO-20-195G, OMB Circular A-94, IRS Publication 946, NIST AI 100-1), the COCOMO II Model Definition Manual, An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in Python, and Sutton & Barto's Reinforcement Learning are free online and linked from the weekly modules. Course notebooks and data packs live in the repository's notebooks/ and data/ folders and open in Colab from each module page. Every link on this site is checked with tools/check_links.py before the term opens.
