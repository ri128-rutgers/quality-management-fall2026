Objectives

  • Compute PV, EV, AC, CPI, SPI, EAC, and TCPI and interpret them for a technology program.
  • Explain how EVM breaks down for agile and ML work and what adaptations (agile EVM, outcome-based measures) are used.

Six things to leave with

  • PV, EV, AC; CPI and SPI; EAC and TCPI — the vocabulary sponsors expect.
  • CPI stabilizes early; use it to forecast, and be suspicious of a program whose CPI never moves.
  • Agile and ML programs need adapted EVM: story points as planned value, experimentation as planned uncertainty.
  • Quality gates and defect trends are the third leg; report them beside CPI/SPI.
  • Model-based EAC forecasting is justified when performance data is rich; otherwise it is theater.
  • Agents default to the simplest EAC formula regardless of CPI trend — verify.
