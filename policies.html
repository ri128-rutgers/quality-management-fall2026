Policies

University-required statements and course rules

Academic integrity

Rutgers University treats cheating, fabrication, plagiarism, denying others access to information or material, and facilitating violations of academic integrity as serious offenses. All students are expected to know and abide by the Rutgers Academic Integrity Policy. Suspected violations in this course are reported to the School of Graduate Studies as the policy requires. In this course specifically: problem sets may be discussed at the conceptual level with classmates, but every submission must be your own work, written and computed by you; the midterm is strictly individual; and the use of generative AI tools must be disclosed as described in the syllabus.

Disability accommodations

Rutgers is committed to full inclusion of students with disabilities. If you have a disability and require accommodations, register with the Office of Disability Services and share your Letter of Accommodation with the instructor as early in the term as possible. Accommodations for the midterm window and final project deadline must be arranged before those dates.

Title IX and reporting

The instructor is a Responsible Employee under Rutgers policy and must report disclosures of sexual misconduct to the Title IX Coordinator. Confidential support is available from the Office for Violence Prevention and Victim Assistance and from Counseling, ADAP and Psychiatric Services (CAPS).

Student wellness and support

Graduate study alongside full-time work is demanding. If you are struggling, contact the instructor early; extensions are far easier to arrange before a deadline than after. University resources include CAPS, the Dean of Students, and the Office of Veteran and Military Programs and Services.

Absence and religious observance

Because the course is asynchronous, short absences require no notification. For religious observances that conflict with a deadline, notify the instructor within the first two weeks of the term and an alternative date will be set in accordance with university policy. For extended illness or emergency, use the University absence reporting system and contact the instructor.

Recording and intellectual property

Course recordings, notebooks, and problem sets are provided for enrolled students' use in this course and may not be redistributed or posted publicly. Public materials on this site are licensed for reuse under the terms in the repository.

Communication

Announcements are posted in Canvas and mirrored by email. The instructor responds to messages within one business day Monday–Friday and does not monitor messages on weekends or university holidays. For grading questions, wait 24 hours after grades post, review the rubric, then message with the specific criterion in question.

Changes to this syllabus

The instructor may adjust readings, deadlines within a week, or point allocations with notice in Canvas. Assessment weights and the final project deadline will not change after the second week of the term except by university action.
