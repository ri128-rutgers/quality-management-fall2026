Live sessions

Thursdays 8:00–9:00 PM ET on Zoom · optional · not graded · recorded

The course is asynchronous. Attendance at the live session is optional and is never part of your grade; nothing is covered live that is not also in the module and the recording. Each week's live session is a working hour: a short walk-through of the trickiest part of the module, open questions on the problem set, and time with the instructor. The Zoom cloud recording (with transcript) is posted in Canvas under the week's module within 24 hours, and the link is added to the announcement for that week.

Dates

Fourteen sessions, every Thursday from September 3 through December 3, 2026, 8:00 PM Eastern. There is no session on December 10 (Week 15) or during the examination period. Thanksgiving Day, November 26, does fall on a Thursday; that session is held as scheduled but is short and optional even by this course's standards.

How to join

The Zoom link, meeting ID, passcode, and calendar file are posted in the Zoom tab and the pinned announcement in Canvas. They are not published on this public site. Add the recurring meeting to your calendar from the .ics file in that announcement.

Zoom problems: contact the Rutgers OIT Help Desk. Use your Rutgers NetID to sign in to Zoom before joining; the meeting requires authentication.

Session format

  1. Ten minutes: the one concept from this week's module most people get wrong.
  2. Twenty minutes: live work on a problem-set-style question with the instructor's workbook or notebook on screen.
  3. Remaining time: your questions, in the order they were posted to the week's Canvas thread, then live.

Post questions to the Canvas thread ahead of time if you cannot attend; they are answered on the recording.
