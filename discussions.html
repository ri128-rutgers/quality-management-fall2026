Weekly discussions

10% of the final grade · seven graded weeks (2, 4, 6, 7, 10, 12, 15) · lowest dropped

Format

In each graded week, post an original response of 100–200 words by Wednesday 11:59 PM ET and reply substantively to one classmate by Friday 11:59 PM ET. Week 1 is the introduction thread (completion credit); Weeks 3, 5, 9, 11, and 14 have no discussion because a case or lab is due; Week 13 (Thanksgiving) is optional. Your lowest graded week is dropped.

Prompts are practical

Each prompt asks for something from your own professional world. Anonymize names, vendors, and non-public figures; describe the structure of the situation. Ungraded threads in other weeks are marked optional on the module page and carry no points.

The seven graded discussions

Week 2 is a professional-example prompt. From Week 4 on, each has a peer-reviewed source at the intersection of quality management, AI, and industrial/systems engineering, an interactive post builder, and reply lenses. Posting happens in Canvas; the same prompt, source, and builder are embedded in each Canvas discussion.
