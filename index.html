Quality Management: quality economics for AI-enabled systems

16:540:580:90 approaches quality management the way a cost review board does: quality is bought with prevention and appraisal spending now or paid for in failure later, and the job is to decide how much and where. The course runs the engineering economy and life-cycle cost toolkit through the quality body of knowledge and applies both to the systems ISEs are actually asked to manage in 2026: software-, cloud-, data-, and AI-intensive. You will use machine learning to estimate cost, and you will cost machine learning.

How the course runs

Plan on three hours a week: 45 minutes of recorded segments, an hour of reading (listed sections only), and 75 minutes of work. Everything computational is a one-click Colab notebook (Azure ML Studio works too); Excel is optional. An optional one-hour Zoom session runs Thursdays at 8:00 PM ET; it is recorded and posted within 24 hours, and attendance is never required or graded — see Live sessions.

Announcements are posted in Canvas and mirrored on the Announcements page. Grades live in Canvas only. Textbooks and how to get them through Rutgers Libraries: Textbooks.
